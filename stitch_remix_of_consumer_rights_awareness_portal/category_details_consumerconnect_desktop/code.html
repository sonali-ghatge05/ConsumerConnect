Faulty Product Remedies

A comprehensive guide on your rights and the legal recourse available when dealing with defective or substandard goods under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

1. The Complaint Process

1

Notice to Seller

Inform the manufacturer or seller about the defect in writing. Clearly state the issue and the remedy you expect (repair, replacement, or refund).

schedule Time Estimate: Within 30 days of purchase or noticing defect
2

Drafting Formal Complaint

If the seller refuses to cooperate, draft a formal complaint. This document should detail the transaction, the defect, and the relief sought, supported by evidence.

schedule Time Estimate: Allow 15 days for seller response before proceeding
3

Filing with Consumer Forum

Submit the complaint to the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (District, State, or National) based on the value of goods/services.

schedule Time Estimate: Must be filed within 2 years from the cause of action
warning

If there's no response

If the seller completely ignores your legal notice after the stipulated time frame, this non-responsiveness strengthens your case at the consumer forum as an 'Unfair Trade Practice'.

3. Available Remedies

  • check_circle
    Removal of Defect Free repair of the product by the manufacturer or authorized service center.
  • check_circle
    Replacement of Goods A completely new product of similar description, free from any defects.
  • check_circle
    Refund of Price Return of the entire amount paid along with applicable interest.
  • check_circle
    Compensation for Loss Damages awarded for any loss, mental agony, or physical injury suffered due to negligence.

Note: Small claims are valid and encouraged under the law. Do not hesitate to file even for minor amounts.

Consumer Protection Act, 2019 Provisions

  • Section 2(10): Defines "defect" as any fault, imperfection, or shortcoming in the quality, quantity, potency, purity, or standard which is required to be maintained by or under any law.
  • Section 2(11): Defines "deficiency" in services, protecting consumers against substandard service related to product repair.
  • Chapter VI (Sections 82-87): Product Liability - Holds manufacturers, sellers, and service providers responsible for compensating for injury or damage caused by defective products.
  • Section 39: Outlines the power of the District Commission to issue orders directing the removal of defects, replacement, or return of price.

Draft Complaint Notice

To, [Name of the Seller/Manager] [Address of the Store/Company] Date: [Date] Subject: Notice regarding defective product [Product Name and Model] Dear Sir/Madam, I purchased a [Product Name] from your store located at [Store Address] on [Date of Purchase] vide invoice number [Invoice Number]. I regret to inform you that the product is defective. Specifically, [Describe the exact defect or issue clearly]. I request you to kindly [replace the defective product / repair the defect / refund the purchase amount of Rs. X] within 15 days of receiving this notice. If my grievance is not resolved within the stipulated time, I will be forced to file a formal complaint before the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Enclosed: 1. Copy of Invoice 2. Copy of Warranty Card Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Address] [Your Contact Number]

Determine jurisdiction based on your claim amount
