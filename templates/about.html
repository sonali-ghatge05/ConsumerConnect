About ConsumerConnect

Know Your Rights. Know Your Options.

ConsumerConnect is an interactive digital resource developed as part of our Community Engagement Project. It aims to improve practical awareness of consumer rights and grievance mechanisms so that citizens can move from knowing their rights to taking informed action.

Why We Created ConsumerConnect

Our primary survey revealed a clear gap between awareness and action.

89%

were aware that consumers have legal rights in India

36%

knew where to actually file a complaint

7%

were aware of the e-Jagriti online filing portal

50%

cited not knowing the process as a major barrier

“Consumers may know that they have rights, but many do not know how to exercise them.”

ConsumerConnect was created to help bridge this awareness-to-action gap by turning complex legal information into clear, practical guidance.

What is ConsumerConnect?

Instead of presenting long legal text, ConsumerConnect organises information into interactive sections that guide users step by step.

How the Project Works

1

Survey

We conducted a primary survey to understand public awareness of consumer rights and grievance mechanisms.

2

Identify Awareness Gaps

Analysis showed that many people know rights exist but do not know how or where to file a complaint.

3

Research Consumer Rights & Laws

We studied the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, official portals, and practical complaint procedures.

4

Design Information Structure

Information was organised into clear categories, processes, documents, remedies, and FAQs for easy understanding.

5

Develop ConsumerConnect

The interactive website was built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Flask, and Jinja2.

6

User Testing & Feedback

The resource is refined based on user feedback to improve clarity and usefulness.
